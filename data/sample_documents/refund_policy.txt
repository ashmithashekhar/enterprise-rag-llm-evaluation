Brightline Software — Subscription Refund Policy (Sample Document)

This is a synthetic sample document written for demonstration purposes. Brightline Software is a fictional company and this policy is illustrative only.

Refund eligibility

Customers on a monthly subscription plan may request a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase or renewal charge. Refund requests submitted after 14 days are evaluated case by case and are normally issued as account credit rather than a payment refund.

Annual plan refunds

Customers on an annual plan may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis for the unused whole months remaining in the term, minus a 10 percent administrative fee.

How to request a refund

To request a refund, the account owner must contact the support team from the email address associated with the account and include the invoice number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after approval. Charges that have already been disputed with a payment provider (chargebacks) are not eligible for a parallel refund.
